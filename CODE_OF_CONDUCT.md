# Code of Conduct

`aliasx` is an open-source project, and everyone taking part in it is covered by this policy.

- We aim for a welcoming, professional space where people can build, learn, and collaborate safely.
- This applies to Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and related project channels.

---

## Expectations

- ✅ Be respectful; assume good intent
- ✅ Be constructive; critique ideas, not people
- ✅ Be inclusive and considerate
- ✅ Keep discussions on-topic and actionable
- ✅ Respect privacy, attribution, and consent
- ✅ Treat external knowledge and contributed skills responsibly

## Not acceptable

- ❌ Harassment, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks
- ❌ Discrimination or exclusionary behavior
- ❌ Sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- ❌ Doxxing or sharing private information
- ❌ Trolling, bad-faith engagement, or deliberate disruption
- ❌ Knowingly contributing malicious, deceptive, poisoned, or unsafe skill content

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, report it privately:

<mailto:comstrx@gmail.com>

Do not report conduct violations in public Issues or Discussions.

For security vulnerabilities, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/comstrx/aliasx/security/advisories/new).

Include what happened, where it happened, who was involved, and any relevant context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

- A request to change tone or content
- Content moderation (edit/remove/lock)
- Rejection or removal of unsafe contributions
- A formal warning
- Temporary restrictions
- Permanent bans for severe or repeated violations
